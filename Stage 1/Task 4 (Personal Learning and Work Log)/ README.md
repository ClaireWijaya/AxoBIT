# AxoBIT — Stage 1 Personal Learning and Work Logs

## Overview

This document compiles the individual **Personal Learning and Work Logs** of the six members of Team AxoBIT for Stage 1 of the Artificial Intelligence and Computational Science course at Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT).

The logs document each member's activities, individual contributions, learning progress, challenges, reflections, next steps, and use of AI tools throughout the project.

- **Selected Module:** Algorithms, Data Structures, and Connectionist AI

## Document Contents

Each member's section records relevant activities and learning progress. Depending on the entry, the logs include:

- **Activity and Purpose:** The task performed and its objective.
- **Individual Contribution:** The member's specific work and responsibilities.
- **Outcome and Evidence:** Results or evidence of completed work.
- **Learning and Reflection:** Knowledge gained, difficulties encountered, and lessons learned.
- **Next Steps:** Tasks or improvements planned.
- **AI Use Records:** The purpose of using AI tools, outcomes, verification, and personal learning.

## Purpose

The purpose of these logs is to document individual participation, support reflection on the learning process, and provide evidence of each member's contributions to Stage 1.

They also help the team track progress, identify unfinished work, and understand how tools such as GitHub and AI-assisted applications were used during project development.
