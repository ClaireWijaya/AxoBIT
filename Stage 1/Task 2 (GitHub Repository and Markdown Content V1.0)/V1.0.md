  Before getting further, what exactly is a computer? We always use it in daily life, but do you really know what it is?
  A computer is a programmable device that stores, retrieves, and processes data. 

  FUN FACT: The word ***‘computer’*** was actually given to HUMANS who were able to perform numerical calculations using a mechanical device such as an abacus. 

  <img width="424" height="200" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1d6979f-7092-4791-8a6b-778428c5c7da" />
  
  Figure 1.2 - Abacus

  The computer itself is made of physical materials, called hardware. Hardware has 4 components to do computation, which are processor, memory, network, and storage. 
  
  1) Processor : Computer's 🧠 (brain), processing data input with algorithms to create output
  
  2) Memory : Address information in a computer and store it in consistent locations
  
  3) Network : The connection between several technological devices which can share information
  
  4) Storage : The hardware that permanently stores a computer's data
  
  Besides hardware, there is something called software. Software consists of programs and instructions written in a programming language (such as Python, C, C++, Java) to process data and produce results. 
  
  A brief history about computer : 

  1) Calculating tool to perform arithmetic → Abacus

#Notes : 

*An algorithm is a sequence of instructions that enables computing. In a simple way, with an algorithm, an input can be processed step by step to create an output. 

*Transistor is a semiconductor device used to amplify or switch electrical signals and power

*Integrated Circuit (IC) is also called a chip, an electronic circuit that is formed by electronic components, such as transistors, resistors, and capacitors, and their interconnections.

*Microprocessor is an integrated circuit (IC) that contains all the functions of a central processing unit (CPU) of a computer.

## 2) A Brief History of Computer Science - How the study of computation became academic discipline 
  -  After the brief computer history, researchers began exploring the principles of computation, algorithms, and programming 
  
  - A Turing-complete computer is what almost all computers are considered into. Where did it come from?

    + 1930s : Alan Mathison Turing is a very important mathematician figure in the development of theoretical computer science who introduced the Turing machine.

    + Turing Machine is a model that explains a set of instructions a machine can follow to process and solve problems.

    + In 1953 → The University of Cambridge introduced a Diploma in Computer Science.
      
    + In 1962 →  Purdue University established the first computer science department in the US

### 3) Computer Science Interdisciplinary
  - Data Science
    
    → Applies computing to extract and manage information from data

      + Do you know about spreadsheets?
        
        Spreadsheet is a simple example about Data Science where data is organized into cells in a table and the table can be visualized in a diagram. However, the shortcoming is that the spreadsheet has a limit of row and column. 
We might think the size limit is already more than enough for common people to use. But think about how a country managed to report cases about Covid-19 back then. Even in 2020, the Public Health England failed to report 15,481 positive cases of Covid-19 due to row limits in the spreadsheet. A large amount of data like this is called Big Data, and data scientists are the ones who analyze it.

    → Studies information technologies and systems that are related to people, organizations, and societies
    
      + With social media, each person in the whole world could connect with each other and have the latest news at the same time. Back then, people used to get information from newspapers, while in modern days, we can get up-to-date information by scrolling through our phone.

### 4) Soooo,

  Computer science influences society through algorithms and Artificial Intelligence. These can help solve problems in areas such as agriculture, healthcare, and education. However, they can also produce errors because of limitations or biases in their data. Therefore, computer scientists should evaluate the effects of their technologies and develop systems that are reliable, fair, and beneficial to society.
